Favorite Three Food Trucks

I have three favorite food trucks located in Philadelphia PA. The three food trucks are different types of food, and I feel like they are all unique in their own way.

Favorite Food Trucks
Name of Food Truck Location of Food Truck Photo of Food Truck Type of Food Offered Why It's My Favorite
La Catrachita 5327 Whitaker Ave, Philadelphia, PA. Mexican Cuisine A lot of places make Birra Tacos, but none of them make it as good as La Catrachita. The Chicken is my favorite, it is 3 for $20.00, but it is always fresh and meaty.
Wokworks 490 N Broad St, Philadelphia, PA. Asian Cuisine Wokworks make stir-fry dishes that are both delicious and healthy. This is my favorite because they offer little tortia chips as a toping and I just love the crunch it adds.
Rock N Rolls 1299 Montgomery Ave, Philadelphia, PA. American & Asian Cuisine Rock N Rolls is my favorite because they mix American and Asian flavors in unique ways, creating a distinctive taste that I can't find anywhere else. I love the sweet chili salmon roll.
